How Ghost Writer Works

1. The Binary Pixel Noise Optical Illusion

Directly inspired by the Bad Apple!! but it disappears if you pause it and Lost in the Static phenomena: The entire canvas is filled with high-density binary black (0) and white (1) noise pixels.

2. Motion vs Static Invariance

When the video is in motion:

  • Inside Secret Text: Pixels flip and cycle randomly at 60 FPS.
  • Background Noise: Pixels stay frozen or update at a calm 2 Hz.

The human visual persistence (POV) integrates the fast transient state changes into a clear, solid perceptual silhouette of the letters.

3. Camouflage on Pause

When paused, all frame-to-frame changes halt immediately. Every pixel remains at its current black or white state with an exact 50/50 random probability across the entire canvas. The text completely vanishes into pure television static.
